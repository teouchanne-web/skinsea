Frequently Asked Questions

Here you’ll find answers to the most frequently asked questions about products, orders, delivery and returns.

Every skin type has different needs. On each product page, you can find information about key ingredients, benefits and recommended use to help you choose the right product.
A serum is generally applied to cleansed skin before moisturizer. The frequency and amount depend on the specific product and its ingredients. Detailed instructions are provided on each product page.
Skinsea's selection is sourced from trusted suppliers. When receiving products, we pay close attention to the packaging, labeling, expiration dates, and other identifying details. If you would like additional information about a specific product, please contact us.
The price of the same product may vary between stores depending on sourcing, suppliers, operating costs and pricing policies. Skinsea is an online store, which allows us to keep operating costs relatively low and offer competitive prices.
Some active ingredients can be combined, but not all products are compatible with each other. Before using several products together, check the recommendations for each product.
The time needed to see results varies and depends on the product, your skin and how consistently it is used. For best results, follow the product instructions.
Check the product description, ingredients and recommendations. If you need help choosing, contact us and our team will help you select a suitable product.
Choose the product you want, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout. Fill in the required information and confirm your order.
Delivery time depends on the delivery address and selected service. You will see the applicable delivery terms during checkout.
Delivery cost depends on the order and delivery address. Delivery is free for orders over 100 GEL.
Products may be returned in accordance with the conditions set out in our Return Policy. For detailed information, see the Return Policy page.
Store the product according to the recommendations on its packaging and keep it away from direct sunlight and high temperatures.
If you have any additional questions, contact us by email: hello@skinsea.ge
We’ll be happy to help you choose the right product and assist with the ordering process.
