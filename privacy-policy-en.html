Privacy Policy

Skinsea respects your privacy and handles users’ personal information responsibly.

1. What information do we collect?

When you place an order or contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and information related to your order.

2. How do we use your information?

We use your information to process orders, arrange delivery, communicate with customers and improve our services.

3. Sharing information with couriers

Information required to deliver your order, such as your name, phone number and delivery address, may be shared with a courier service provider solely for the purpose of completing the delivery.

4. Data protection

We take reasonable measures to protect users’ personal information and use it only for the purposes for which it was collected.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell users’ personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant service.

6. Your rights

You may contact us with questions about your personal information or to request that your information be updated or deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

Skinsea may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The updated version will be published on this page.
